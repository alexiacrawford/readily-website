PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Readily respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected and used when you use the Readily mobile application and related services.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Readily, we may collect information such as your email address, account information, reading-list information, and approximate or precise location when you grant location permission.

How We Use Information

We use information to provide and improve Readily, authenticate users, display book availability, identify nearby bookstores and libraries, maintain your preferences, and provide support.

Location Information

Readily may request access to your device location to identify bookstores and other book sources near you. Location access is only used for features that depend on your location and is subject to the permissions you grant through your device.

Third-Party Services

Readily may use third-party services to provide features such as authentication, infrastructure, book information, and store availability. Those services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

Data Security

We use reasonable safeguards designed to protect information associated with Readily. No electronic storage or transmission system can guarantee absolute security.

Your Choices

You may manage permissions such as location access through your device settings. You may also contact us regarding questions about your account or personal information.

Children's Privacy

Readily is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Readily evolves. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact us at support@readilybooks.com.
